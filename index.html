About the atlas

This pilot presents the Lombardia portion of a national-scale GIS framework for ground-mounted photovoltaic land restrictions in Italy.

The underlying research integrates six thematic groups of spatial constraints and evaluates where multiple restrictions overlap.

Constraint groups

Landscape assets
Landscape-protected areas and related spatial restrictions.
Cultural heritage
Protected cultural and heritage assets and their applicable buffers.
Natural assets
Natura 2000, EUAP, IBA and Ramsar protected areas.
Environmental constraints
Environmental safety constraints represented in the analysis, including water-body related restrictions.
Infrastructure
Safety distances around infrastructure and built-up areas.
Natural hazards
Areas affected by flood and landslide hazard restrictions.

Research basis

Ranjgar, B.; Niccolai, A.; Leva, S. (2025). Where Can Solar Go? Assessing Land Availability for PV in Italy Under Regulatory Constraints. Solar, 5, 40.
